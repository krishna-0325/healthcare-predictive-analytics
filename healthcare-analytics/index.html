Ethical Considerations
Data Anonymization
All patient records in training datasets are de-identified in compliance with HIPAA standards. No personally identifiable information (name, SSN, contact) is stored, transmitted, or used in model training.
Bias Mitigation
Models are evaluated across demographic subgroups. The Pima dataset has known demographic limitations (female patients of Pima Indian heritage) — results may not generalize universally across all populations.
Model Explainability
SHAP values and feature importance scores are used to ensure predictions are interpretable by clinicians. No black-box outputs — every prediction is accompanied by a feature contribution breakdown.
Clinical Validation
Predictive outputs must be reviewed by a licensed healthcare professional. This tool is designed to assist, not replace, clinical decision-making. Always consult your doctor.
Continuous Improvement
Models are retrained periodically with new data. Performance metrics are publicly disclosed. Any degradation in minority subgroup performance triggers a bias audit before redeployment.
Informed Consent
In clinical settings, patients must provide informed consent before their data is used for predictive analysis. Data usage policies must be clearly communicated and opt-out must be available.
